Glossary

Plaintext
The original, readable message.
Ciphertext
The scrambled message after encryption.
Key
The secret value that decides how the message is scrambled.
Encoding
A change of format (like Base64) that gives no secrecy at all.
Made for a Cryptography assignment. The old ciphers are for learning; real data needs AES.
